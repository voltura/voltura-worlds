# Sun

Sun appears immediately after Pluto in the library and tray visualization menu.
It works offline, on each display independently, with Random, Apply to all,
saved settings, and exported/imported World presets.

The base is a NASA/SDO AIA 171 Å observation from September 24, 2026 at
00:06:10 UTC. Orange depicts extreme ultraviolet intensity in false color,
not the Sun's visible-light color. Real 304 Å prominence structure from March 30, 2010 is composited with this disk. The shader animates small, independently circulating surface patches and local prominence flow and emission
while preserving the front-facing observation; it is not a live feed or a
scientific simulation. See [source and processing credits](../assets/sun/ATTRIBUTION.md).

| Control | Effect |
|---|---|
| Brightness | Emission and glow across the entire visualization; zero is black |
| Movement | Speed of plasma, loops, streamers and camera drift; zero freezes motion and audio effects |
| Surface detail | Fine photographic contrast; the broad NASA structure remains at zero |
| Camera drift | Gentle composition movement; zero fixes the camera |
| Solar activity | Observed off-limb prominences, local surface flow and evolving emission cells; zero removes the added activity |
| Corona | Observed off-limb emission and surrounding streamer envelope; zero preserves the disk |
| Audio strength | Emission/activity response when Gentle or Expressive reaction is enabled |
| Palette | SDO orange, Golden corona, Deep amber, Neutral intensity — all false color |

Existing parameter defaults apply. Audio reaction starts Off. Eco reduces prominence-noise complexity while preserving the source texture. The solar disk occupies 64%
of the display's shorter edge, leaving space for the corona on all aspect ratios.
