# Voltura Worlds 0.4.0

Tropical Paradise brings the library to 24 built-in Worlds. A photorealistic jungle frames a sunlit waterfall and clear turquoise pool, with native GPU animation for falling water, pool ripples, drifting mist and gently moving foliage.

Choose Natural, Warm sunlight, Cool lagoon or Soft overcast palettes, and adjust waterfall flow, water ripples, water detail and foliage breeze. Optional audio reaction adds subtle pool disturbance. Tropical Paradise is available in the library, desktop and tray menus, presets and Random rotation.

Palette labels now refresh correctly when switching between scenes that share a Natural palette name.

The app, installer, desktop-menu DLL, identity package and certificate-trust helper are signed and timestamped. Voltura's self-signed certificate is not publicly trusted. Run the installer to upgrade while preserving settings, imported videos and startup preferences.
