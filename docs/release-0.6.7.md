# Voltura Worlds 0.6.7

**Swedish Christmas** brings the Voltura Julkalender’s snowy cottage and lit tree to the Windows desktop. The scene starts at night, reaches full daylight over 15 minutes of active playback, and stays in daylight with falling snow and animated tree lights. Pausing holds the transition. Switching away and back restarts it at night.

Play the preview on the [Voltura Worlds product page](https://worlds.voltura.se/#christmas-preview) or find it behind door 24 of the [Voltura Julkalender](https://x-mas.voltura.se/).
