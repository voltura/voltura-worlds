# Swedish Christmas artwork

The two aligned landscape plates were generated with the built-in OpenAI image generation tool for the Voltura Julkalender and Voltura Worlds. The daylight plate is a relighting of the night plate. Both show the same Falu red cottage, snowy fir forest, path, and Christmas tree.

Worlds blends the plates in linear light and renders snowfall, drifting chimney smoke, individual tree-light twinkles, and ice-crystal glints in its native D3D12 renderer. The preview cuts between native captures at night, dawn, and daylight.
