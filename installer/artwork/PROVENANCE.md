# Worlds installer artwork

`worlds-sidebar.png` is original artwork generated for Voltura Worlds on 2026-10-25 using OpenAI image generation. It is branding artwork, not a screenshot or a claim about native scene rendering. `worlds-sidebar.bmp` is the RGB, 1312 × 2512 installer-format conversion.

`worlds.ico` and `worlds-icon.png` are an original geometric orbital mark created for this application. The ICO includes 16, 20, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, and 256 pixel images.

Regenerate the icons with `scripts/build-icons.ps1`. The enlarged mark draws the far ring behind the opaque planet and the near ring in front, matching the notification-area geometry in `createTrayIcon` in `src/main.cpp`.
